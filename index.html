How to Play

  1. Choose a difficulty mode.
  2. Press Shuffle to begin.
  3. Click tiles next to the empty space to slide them.
  4. Rearrange the tiles until the image is complete.
  5. Complete the puzzle using the fewest moves and fastest time possible.

Difficulty Modes

  • Easy: Includes hints and shows correctly placed tiles.
  • Regular: Includes limited hints but no tile position feedback.
  • Hard: No hints or placement assistance.

About the 15 Puzzle

The 15 Puzzle is a classic sliding puzzle invented in the late 1800s. It consists of fifteen numbered or image tiles arranged in a 4×4 grid with one empty space.

Players must slide tiles around the board until the original arrangement is restored. The puzzle became one of the most popular mechanical puzzles of its era.

Fun Facts

  • Not every possible arrangement can be solved.
  • A valid shuffle must preserve the puzzle's mathematical rules.
  • The number of possible board arrangements is greater than 20 trillion.
  • Strategy and planning are often more important than speed.
